      How to read this page

      Prices come from CoinGecko and are published as one shared hourly snapshot for every page. PEP supply comes from the dated chain snapshot. If an update fails, the last saved values keep their original measurement time.

      Why Pepe (PEPE)? PEPE is a different token, on Ethereum, with a similar name and a much bigger market cap. Many people looking for one find the other. The overlay shows whether the two moved together in the period you pick; moving together in the past says nothing about the future. Which frog is which.

      Market cap. Price × the number of PEP that exist, about 105.2 billion. PEP’s supply grows by 10,000 PEP a block, so the same market cap means a slightly lower price per PEP over time. Supply and inflation.
