Mined, not minted

One machine. Three coins.

Litecoin, Dogecoin and Pepecoin can be mined together on the same Scrypt work, through merged mining (AuxPoW). Same machine, same electricity, three payouts.

  • A Scrypt ASIC on a pool that merge-mines PEP earns all three
  • No validators, no staking, no contract owner
  • Nobody can freeze an address at the chain level

Get PEP in four steps

The full version, with every exchange and the common mistakes, is on How to buy PEP.

  1. Open a Kraken accountBuy on the PEP/USD or PEP/EUR market. PEP is Pepecoin with its own blockchain, not PEPE.
  2. Withdraw to your own walletSend your PEP to an address that starts with P. Not your keys, not your frog.
  3. Get a Tangem, if you likeA hardware card that keeps PEP offline. The official Pepecoin Shop sells a co-branded one.
  4. HODL and say gmDrop by r/pepecoin or the Discord and feel the vibe.
