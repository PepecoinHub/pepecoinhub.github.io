    How to read this

    • The robot has saved a snapshot every day since 5 Oct 2026. Everything before that was rebuilt from the blockchain itself, back to the first mined block on 30 Jan 2024: one list a day for the first week, one a week from 5 Feb 2024 to 25 Aug 2025 and one a day from 1 Sep 2025. The top 10 to top 1000 shares were rebuilt for every single day.
    • An address is not a person. Exchanges, pools and custodians hold coins for many users. The view without exchanges and pools is a closer picture of everyone else, but the tags are incomplete.
    • All shares are measured against the circulating supply on the day of the snapshot, so a growing supply alone lowers a share a little each day.
    • Each day uses that day’s top addresses, so the group changes as wallets climb or fall.
