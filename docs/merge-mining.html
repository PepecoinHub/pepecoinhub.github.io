Merge-mine Pepecoin with your LTC and DOGE miner

If your Scrypt ASIC mines Litecoin and Dogecoin on a big pool, it is most likely working on Pepecoin blocks too. Pepecoin (PEP) is merge-mined: the same hashes, no extra power, a third coin. The real question is whether your pool pays it to you.

Not investment advice. At today’s price the dollar amount is small; what it costs you is one address in your pool settings.

Claim it in three steps

  1. Get a PEP address

    Any Pepecoin wallet works, such as Pepecoin Core or a Tangem card. A PEP address starts with P. An Ethereum address (0x…) will not work: PEP is not PEPE. PEP wallets.

  2. Paste it into your pool

    Open the payout or merged-mining settings on your pool’s website and add the address for Pepecoin. On Binance Pool this step decides whether you get any PEP at all.

  3. Leave the miner as it is

    No new pool URL, no new worker, no firmware change. PEP arrives next to your LTC and DOGE once it passes the pool’s minimum payout.

Why your pool can mine PEP for free

Pepecoin is a Scrypt coin built on Dogecoin’s code, with AuxPoW (merged mining) switched on at block 42,000. When a Litecoin pool builds its block, it can tuck in a link to a Dogecoin block and a Pepecoin block. Every share your miner sends is checked against all three targets. When one is good enough for Pepecoin’s difficulty, the pool finds a PEP block, and nothing is taken away from Litecoin or Dogecoin. That is why the big Litecoin pools added PEP in late 2024.

Hold DOGE rather than mine it? PEP is its closest relative: the same code base, the same merged mining, a frog instead of a dog, and no premine. PEP and DOGE side by side.

Common questions

Does merge mining PEP lower my Litecoin or Dogecoin rewards?

No. The work is not split between chains: the same hashes are checked against each coin’s target. In ViaBTC’s words, PEP is an additional reward from merged mining that needs no extra hashrate.

Which coins can I merge-mine with Litecoin?

Dogecoin, plus smaller AuxPoW coins such as Pepecoin (PEP), Bellscoin, Luckycoin, Junkcoin, Dingocoin and Shibacoin. Which ones you are paid depends on the pool.

Is Dogecoin merge mining the same as mining Pepecoin?

Both DOGE and PEP are merge-mined with Litecoin as the parent chain. A pool that includes both pays them separately. Mining DOGE gives you PEP only if your pool also merge-mines Pepecoin and pays it out.

I mine on Binance Pool. Why do I see no PEP?

Binance Pool pays PEP only to accounts that have a Pepecoin payout address; without one, nothing is credited. Its minimum payout is 20,000 PEP (announcement of 23 December 2024).

Do I need to change my miner’s settings?

No. On a pool that merge-mines PEP the stratum address and worker stay the same. You only set the PEP payout address on the pool’s website.

Can an Antminer L7 or L9 merge-mine Pepecoin?

Yes. Any Scrypt ASIC that mines Litecoin and Dogecoin can, including the Antminer L7 and L9 and the ElphaPex DG1. The pool does the merged mining.

Can I merge-mine PEP solo, with my own node?

Pepecoin Core is a fork of Dogecoin Core and has the AuxPoW RPC calls createauxblock and submitauxblock, which merge-mining pool software uses. It is a setup for experienced operators; for most miners a pool is simpler.

Is it worth it?

In dollars, not much at today’s price: the calculator above uses the latest PEP price. It costs no extra power or hashrate, so the only cost is adding one address. PEP you mine is yours to keep or sell.
