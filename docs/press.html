Description to copy

One sentence

Pepecoin (PEP) is a cryptocurrency with its own proof-of-work blockchain, launched on January 30, 2024 and merge-mined with Litecoin and Dogecoin. It is not the Pepe (PEPE) token on Ethereum.

One paragraph

Pepecoin (PEP) is the native coin of its own blockchain, a fork of Dogecoin Core launched on January 30, 2024. It is secured by Scrypt proof of work and, since block 42,000, by merged mining with Litecoin and Dogecoin, so the same mining machines secure all three networks. According to the project’s MiCA whitepaper, there was no premine, no pre-allocation and no ICO: every coin was issued to miners. Each block adds 10,000 PEP, about once a minute, and there is no supply cap. Pepecoin is a different asset from Pepe (PEPE) and PepeCoin (PEPECOIN), which are both tokens on Ethereum.

Naming: which “Pepe” is which

Three different assets go by similar names. Price sites and search results mix them up all the time.

Name and tickerWhat it isAddressSince
Pepecoin (PEP)Its own proof-of-work blockchain. The subject of this site.starts with P2024
Pepe (PEPE)ERC-20 token on Ethereum0x6982…19332023
PepeCoin (PEPECOIN)ERC-20 token on Ethereum; ran its own chain from 20160xA9E8…489A2016
  • On first mention write Pepecoin (PEP), then “Pepecoin” or “PEP”.
  • Price pages: CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap, both under the ticker PEP.
  • Not “Pepe coin”, “Pepe” or “PEPE” for Pepecoin. Most readers will take that to mean the Ethereum token.
  • Not “PepeCoin” for PEP. That spelling belongs to the project from 2016.
  • No contract address. PEP is not a token, so a “PEP contract” on Ethereum or BNB Chain is not Pepecoin.

News feeds on price sites sometimes show stories about other Pepe tokens on the PEP page. Check the ticker before quoting. Side by side, with sources: PEP vs PEPE vs PEPECOIN.

How the figures are made

  • Every day a GitHub Actions job saves the block height, the supply and the top 2,000 addresses from the PepeBlocks explorer and the pepecoinservice.org API. History before October 5, 2026 was rebuilt by replaying the blockchain.
  • Hashrate is the network’s own estimate, read from PepeBlocks. Single readings swing by ±10% within hours.
  • An address is not a person. Exchanges and mining pools hold coins for many people; the rich list tags the known ones.
  • Price and market cap come from CoinGecko. Market cap is simply the price times the supply.

Claims that need context

“No premine”

This is the project’s own statement in its MiCA whitepaper. It matches the chain: the supply shown by the explorers equals the sum of the block-reward schedule (supply page).

“Mined with Litecoin and Dogecoin”

True at the protocol level since block 42,000. Not every Litecoin pool pays out PEP; see which pools do.

“The top 10 hold X%”

These are addresses, not people. Some of the largest belong to exchanges and pools (rich list).

“MiCA whitepaper”

A disclosure document filed so PEP can trade in the EU. It is not an approval or endorsement by any regulator (what MiCA is).
