Summary

  1. What it is. Pepecoin (PEP) is the native coin of its own proof-of-work blockchain, a fork of Dogecoin Core, live since January 30, 2024. It is not a token on Ethereum.Pepecoin Core (GitHub) · MiCA whitepaper, D.8
  2. Who secures it. Since block 42,000 it is merge-mined with Litecoin and Dogecoin: the same Scrypt machines secure all three networks at no extra energy cost.chainparams.cpp · How to mine PEP
  3. How it launched. No premine, no pre-allocation and no ICO, according to the project’s own MiCA whitepaper. Pepecoin LLC (United States) states that it holds no PEP.MiCA whitepaper, D.9 · MiCA whitepaper, A.1, G.5
  4. Supply. 10,000 new PEP per block, about one block a minute, with no cap, the same rule Dogecoin has used since 2015. Inflation is about 5% a year and falls every year.chainparams.cpp · MiCA whitepaper, G.13 · Supply page
  5. Where it trades. On Kraken since February 11, 2026 (PEP/USD, PEP/EUR), with a MiCA whitepaper for the EU, and on several smaller exchanges.Kraken announcement · MiCA

On first mention: Pepecoin (PEP). Not “Pepe coin”, “Pepe” or “PEPE”, which most readers take to mean the Ethereum token.

Possible story angles

  1. Three coins, one name

    Pepecoin (PEP), Pepe (PEPE) and PepeCoin (PEPECOIN) are three different assets that price sites and search results mix up. A clear case of how naming confusion works in crypto.

  2. A meme coin built like Dogecoin

    In a market of tokens issued with one click, PEP is a separate blockchain secured by Litecoin and Dogecoin miners. The question is whether that model still works in 2026.

  3. A fair launch on the record

    The “no premine” claim is filed in an EU regulatory document and can be checked on chain: the supply equals the sum of the block rewards. Holder concentration is published daily as open data.

Description to copy

One sentence

Pepecoin (PEP) is a cryptocurrency with its own proof-of-work blockchain, launched on January 30, 2024 and merge-mined with Litecoin and Dogecoin. It is not the Pepe (PEPE) token on Ethereum.

One paragraph

Pepecoin (PEP) is the native coin of its own blockchain, a fork of Dogecoin Core launched on January 30, 2024. It is secured by Scrypt proof of work and, since block 42,000, by merged mining with Litecoin and Dogecoin, so the same mining machines secure all three networks. According to the project’s MiCA whitepaper, there was no premine, no pre-allocation and no ICO: every coin was issued to miners. Each block adds 10,000 PEP, about once a minute, and there is no supply cap. Pepecoin is a different asset from Pepe (PEPE) and PepeCoin (PEPECOIN), which are both tokens on Ethereum.
