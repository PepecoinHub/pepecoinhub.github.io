How to buy Pepecoin (PEP)

PEP is the coin of the Pepecoin blockchain. Most exchanges trade it against USDT, and Kraken also against US dollars and euros. Buying takes four steps and ends with your PEP in a wallet you control.

Step by step

  1. Pick a market

    Choose an exchange or swap service that lists PEP, such as the ones below. Kraken lists three different assets with Pepe in the name: PEP is the one called Pepecoin, with its own blockchain. How to tell them apart.

  2. Fund your account and buy

    Deposit money or USDT under the exchange's own rules, then buy on the PEP/USD, PEP/EUR or PEP/USDT market.

  3. Withdraw to your own wallet

    In the withdrawal form, pick the Pepecoin network and paste a receiving address from your wallet. PEP addresses start with P. The exchange sets its own withdrawal fee and minimum. Which wallet to use.

  4. Check it in a block explorer

    Paste your address or the transaction ID into PepeBlocks or Pepeplorer and watch it confirm. A new block arrives about once a minute.

Common questions

Which network do I pick when I withdraw PEP?

Pepecoin (PEP). The receiving address must start with P. An address that starts with 0x belongs to Ethereum, a different network.

Is PEP the same as PEPE or PEPECOIN?

No. PEPE and PEPECOIN are tokens on Ethereum. PEP is the coin of its own blockchain. See Which Pepecoin is which?

Can I buy PEP with a card?

Alchemy Pay is listed on pepecoin.com for buying with a card. Exchanges set their own deposit options, so check the exchange you use.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Pepecoin adds a block about once a minute. How many confirmations an exchange waits for, and how fast it sends withdrawals, is up to the exchange.
