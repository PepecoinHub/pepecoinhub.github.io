    How to read this page

    Where the price comes from. CoinGecko's public API, loaded in your browser and reused for up to 10 minutes. PEP trades on few markets with low volume, so the price can jump on small orders and can differ between exchanges.

    Why Pepe (PEPE)? PEPE is a different token, on Ethereum, with a similar name and a much bigger market cap. Many people looking for one find the other. The overlay shows whether the two moved together in the period you pick; moving together in the past says nothing about the future. Which Pepecoin is which.

    Market cap. Price × the number of PEP that exist, about 104.8 billion. PEP's supply grows by 10,000 PEP a block, so the same market cap means a slightly lower price per PEP over time, and a ×100 in price would need a bit more than ×100 in market cap later on. Supply and inflation.
