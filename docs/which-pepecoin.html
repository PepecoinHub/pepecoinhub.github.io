Pepecoin (PEP) in numbers

Genesis
Network live January 30, 2024. The genesis block embeds a Wall Street Journal headline dated January 22, 2024. MiCA white paper; chainparams.cpp
Allocation
None. Every PEP came from block rewards to miners. Pepecoin LLC holds no PEP. MiCA white paper, sections D.9 and G.5
Mining
Scrypt proof of work. Merged mining (AuxPoW) with Litecoin and Dogecoin since block 42,000, chain ID 63. chainparams.cpp
Blocks
About one every 60 seconds, 10,000 PEP each, no supply cap. MiCA white paper, section G.13
Difficulty
40.6M on October 5, 2026. Dogecoin ranged 28.8M–47.5M from September 28 to October 4. Both target 60-second blocks, so PEP runs on hashrate in the same range as Dogecoin's. Pepeplorer; CoinWarz
New coins per day
14.4M PEP ≈ $1,300 to miners, next to 14.4M DOGE ≈ $1.4M. Merged mining adds PEP at no extra hardware or power cost. Block rewards × CoinMarketCap prices, October 5, 2026
Pools
Binance Pool, ViaBTC, F2Pool and EMCD offer PEP alongside LTC and DOGE. Pool announcements and pages, linked below
Supply
~104.8 billion PEP per the Pepeplorer explorer. Some aggregators still show ~102.9 billion. Pepeplorer; CoinMarketCap
Regulation
MiCA white paper for admission to trading on Kraken in the EU, notified April 7, 2026, published May 8, 2026. pepecoin.com/mica-whitepaper.xhtml
Code
MIT-licensed, public at github.com/pepecoinppc/pepecoin.
