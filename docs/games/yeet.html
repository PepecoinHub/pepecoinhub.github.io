Readme.txt — How to become airborne

Aim high. Land questionably.

  1. Choose an angle. Hold Space or the big launch button to charge the power meter, then release.
  2. During flight, press Space or tap FLAP. You get three boosts: save them for a low flight.
  3. Land on red trampolines. Catch the HOPIUM fans. Skateboard Doge helps; mud does not.
  4. Every round the track gets new things. A green PUMP candle launches you high and gives one flap back. A red SELL WALL stops any frog flying lower than its top: flap over it!
  5. Your browser remembers guest records. Log in with Discord to save your record across devices and join the global top 10.

P pauses, R restarts. Sound starts muted. There are no PEP payouts.

Leaderboard scores have plausibility checks and rate limits; this casual browser game is not cheat-proof.
