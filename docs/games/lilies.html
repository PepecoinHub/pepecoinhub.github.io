How to play

  • You get exactly 10 flies. Put them on Lily A, Lily B and Lily C, any whole numbers that add up to 10.
  • Each lily goes to whoever put more flies on it. The same number on both sides: that lily stays undecided.
  • Win two lilies and you win the duel. 1:1 or 0:0 is a draw. Total flies do not matter, only lilies.
  • Create a challenge and walk away: it waits 48 hours for a brave fren. Nobody sees your flies before the duel.
  • Or accept somebody’s challenge: place your flies and the result comes at once, with both spreads.
  • Up to 3 open challenges at a time, 5 new ones a day. No stakes, no PEP, only bragging rights.

Example: 2/4/4 against 4/3/3. Lily A goes to 4, lilies B and C go to 4 over 3. 2/4/4 wins 2:1.

Lily pads and flies are temporary placeholders, waiting for the final art.
