Game rules

Free games, just for fun. Be kind, play fair, and read how points and prizes work.

The short version

  • Everything here is free to play. Nobody has to pay, deposit or send anything to join.
  • One person, one account. No bots, no extra accounts, no sharing answers.
  • Be kind. Hosts and room moderators can remove anyone who spams, harasses or ruins the game.
  • We never ask you to send PEP or any other coin, and we never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is a scammer.

Games and scoring

  • Draw & Guess is casual. Anyone can open a room. Points exist only until the game ends; there is no leaderboard.
  • Guess the Picture is the weekly game night run by a host, and the only mode that counts for the season leaderboard. On each picture, the first 3 correct guesses score 3, 2 and 1 points. Ties are broken by more 1st places, then more 2nd places, then who reached the score first.
  • Table games (Chess, Checkers, Connect Four, Sea Battle) are casual and have no ranking. Every move is checked by the server. A clock or move limit counts as a loss when it runs out, a player who is away for more than 3 minutes forfeits, and leaving a game in progress counts as a loss.
  • Play vs bot runs only in your browser. Nothing is saved or ranked.

Fair play

  • No second accounts or extra nicknames to collect points. Guests are ranked by nickname and marked guest; Discord-verified players are marked ✓.
  • No telling others the secret word or answer, and no letters or words drawn on the board.
  • No offensive nicknames, drawings or chat. A simple filter hides some words, but it cannot catch everything, so the hosts decide.
  • Hosts can remove players and close rooms. Their decision is final.

Prizes

There are no prizes right now. If a game night offers a prize or PEP tips, it is announced in writing, with the rules, before the event starts, on Reddit and Discord. Prizes are decided by the leaderboard rules above. A winner gives an address of their own, and the host sends the prize from the host's own wallet and publishes the transaction. Giving an address is always optional and never required to play.

Problems

Found a bug, a cheater or something unfair? Report it on GitHub or tell a host on Discord. See also the privacy notice.
