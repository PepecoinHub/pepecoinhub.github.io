PEPE SWEEPERfind the safe lily pads

How to play: click a lily pad to look under it. A number tells you how many wrong Pepes hide on the pads around it. Right click (or F) plants a flag where you think one hides. Click a number when it has that many flags around it to open the rest. On a phone, switch between DIG and FLAG, or hold a pad to flag it. Your first click is always safe. Your best times stay in this browser.
