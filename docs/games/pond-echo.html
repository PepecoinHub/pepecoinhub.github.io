Readme.txt — How not to swim

Get Pepe (and the little one on his back) to the far bank.

  1. Drag back from anywhere on the pond and let go, like a slingshot. The ring shows where you land.
  2. Every landing sends a wave. Waves push lilies away from where they start. Use them, or watch the next leaf drift out of reach.
  3. Jumping off a lily pushes it back; landing pushes it on. Fall in the water and you make the biggest wave of all.
  4. After a try, turn on an echo: a see-through Pepe replays that run next to you. Its waves really move your lilies. Wait for it, jump with it, or use the spot where it fell in.
  5. Share your echo. Even a fall. Someone else might cross on your splash, and the replay will show both your names.

Prototype: one crossing, no accounts, nothing leaves your browser except the link you choose to share. No PEP payouts.
