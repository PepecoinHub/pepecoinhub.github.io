    Rules of the pond
    • The king holds three secret moves. You pick three. They play round by round in the order the king set them: guess the pattern.
    • Three rounds: 🪨 beats ✂️, ✂️ beats 📄, 📄 beats 🪨. Win more rounds than the king and the throne is yours. Ties keep the king.
    • Long Live the King: win 10 duels in a row, holding the throne against 10 challengers in one reign, and frens logged on with Discord get a stamp in their Fren Passport plus the King Fren avatar for their Fren.
    • Crown Breaker: dethrone a king who already defended the throne at least 13 times in one reign. Discord frens earn a Fren Passport stamp and the Crown Breaker knight avatar.
    • One duel every 4 hours. Lost? Your frog is bruised: rest 4 hours, then take on the same king again, or whoever sits on the throne by then. Without a Discord log-on the 4 hours count per network too.
    • As king, your three moves defend you while you are away. Come back any time to change them.
    • No sign-in needed. Logged on to the pond (Discord or guest)? Then your throne follows your account to every device, and Discord kings get a Fren Passport link.
    • Your name, your moves and a scrambled network fingerprint are stored to run the game. No PEP, no prizes: only glory. New kings and long reigns make the Pond Bulletin.
