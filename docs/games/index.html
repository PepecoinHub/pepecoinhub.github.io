♟️ Table games

Chess, Checkers, Connect Four and Sea Battle for two players. Open a table, send the invite link, or sit down at an open table below. Everyone else can watch and chat. Or play against the bot (Easy, Medium, Hard). Just for fun: no ranking. Every move is checked by the server.

✏️ Draw & Guess casual

Everyone takes turns drawing. The drawer gets a secret word (simple stuff: cat, pizza, rocket…) and the others type guesses in the chat. Faster guesses score more, and the drawer earns points for every correct guess. Letter hints appear as time runs out. Anyone can start a room.

🖼️ Guess the Picture ranked

After a 3-2-1 countdown the host reveals a picture: first a few giant pixels, then sharper and sharper. First 3 correct guesses score 3 / 2 / 1 points; the round ends after the 3rd correct guess or 2 minutes. The host picks the pictures and answers. Weekly game night, counts for the season.

Community games, run by a volunteer from the r/pepecoin community. Free to play, just for fun. Not an official Pepecoin publication.
