WEAPON PACKS

The classic arsenal is always in. Each ticked pack adds its weapons to both teams.

The goal

Two teams of frogs take turns. Knock the other team out: blasts, falls, or a swim in the pond. Last team standing wins.

Your turn

30 seconds to walk a little, pick a weapon and fire. After a shot you get 3 seconds to run for cover.

Controls

A/D or ← → move · W/S or ↑ ↓ aim
SPACE jumps · SPACE ×2 backflips
RIGHT CLICK: arsenal · HOLD LEFT CLICK / ENTER: fire

TAP OR DRAG ON THE MAP: aim · HOLD FIRE: charge, let go to shoot
◀ ▶ walk · ↑ jump · ↑ ×2 backflip · ▲ weapon button: arsenal

Weapons

  • Thrown and fired (bazooka, grenades, feet, bell, pickaxe): hold to charge, the dotted arc shows the flight.
  • From the sky (Silver Donkey, Pep Rain, Red Candles): pick a spot on the map, then fire. No power needed.
  • Doge Flyer: steer it for 5 seconds, then it blows up, or detonate it early.
  • Miner's Pickaxe: hits the first frog it touches. A miss digs in and flies back; catch it to keep it.
  • Rope: the line shows where the hook catches. Swing, climb, let go.

Camera

Pinch or scroll to zoom, + / − / ⤢ in the corner. The camera follows whatever is moving.
