Sub Says: rules and privacy

Sub Says is a daily question game played inside posts on Reddit communities that install it. This page is its rules and its privacy notice.

How the game works

  • Every round has a question, for example "Name something you'd bring to a picnic". During the answering window (24 hours by default, set by the community's moderators) each player gives exactly 3 different short answers, once.
  • Then comes the guessing window of the same length. The board is hidden. Each player guesses one answer at a time, sees at once whether it is on the board, and has 3 tries. The whole board shows after the last try.
  • An answer reaches the board only if at least 3 different people gave it. Spelling variants, plurals and small typos are merged. Links, contact details and offensive words are rejected, and moderators can hide any answer.
  • Points: 10 for sending your 3 answers. A crowd bonus: each of your answers that reaches the board earns half of its percentage. For guessing, each guess that matches a board answer is worth the percentage of players who gave that answer (the top answer is usually worth the most). Weekly standings reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. A streak counts days in a row with at least one action.
  • If too few people answered, or the answers are too scattered to make a board, the round is skipped and the app says so.
  • Free to play. Nobody has to pay or deposit anything. The app itself gives no prizes. If a community's moderators offer a prize, they set and announce its rules themselves.
  • One person, one account. The community's moderators can hide answers, disqualify cheating and decide in unclear cases.

What the app stores

  • Your Reddit username, so your points can be shown in the weekly standings.
  • Your answers and guesses. They are kept to build and score the board and are never shown next to your name. Only answers given by at least 3 people appear, and only as part of the board. While you type, the app may suggest spellings of answers that other players already gave, without names or counts.
  • Your points, your streak and the date of your last action.

The app stores nothing else: no email address, no IP address, no device information, no payment details. The weekly standings show usernames and points to everyone who opens the game.

Who processes the data

The app runs on Reddit's developer platform (Devvit) and has no server of its own. The data above is kept in the app's storage on Reddit's servers, separately for each community that installs it. Reddit's own privacy policy applies to your use of Reddit.

Your rights

To ask what the app holds about you, or to have your username, answers and points removed, send a direct message to u/byylu on Reddit. Removing your data also removes you from the standings. Sub Says is made by an independent developer and is not an official Reddit product. It is a survey-guessing game: it is not affiliated with any TV show or game brand. Not investment advice.
