Privacy notice

We keep as little as possible. We never ask for your email or your wallet to play.

What is stored

  • A nickname you choose, and a random guest account ID. If Discord login is switched on, your Discord user ID and name are stored too. We do not receive your email or password.
  • Game data: rooms and tables, moves, scores, results and chat messages. Old chat messages are deleted automatically after about a week. Closed tables are removed with their chat after about an hour.
  • A salted hash of your IP address, used only to limit how many rooms one network can open. It cannot be turned back into the address, and the raw address is not saved.
  • Leaderboard: your nickname and points from Guess the Picture nights stay on the leaderboard and in the Hall of Fame.
  • Prize addresses: if prizes are offered and you choose to give a PEP address, it is used only to send that prize.

What stays in your browser

Your nickname, theme choice, the guest sign-in token, and the position of a game against the bot are kept in your browser's local storage so the page remembers you. Games against the bot are never sent to a server. Clearing your site data removes all of this.

Who processes data

  • GitHub Pages serves the pages.
  • Supabase runs the game database and live connection. The project is set up in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Discord handles the login if you choose it.
  • Google Fonts delivers the page font, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • GoatCounter counts page views without cookies or personal profiles, the same as on the rest of Pepecoin Hub.

Cleaning up and your rights

Inactive guest accounts are cleared out from time to time. To ask what we hold about you, or to have a nickname and its scores removed, open an issue on GitHub or message a host on Discord.
