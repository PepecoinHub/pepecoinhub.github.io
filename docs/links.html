Common questions

What is the official Pepecoin website?

pepecoin.com. It lists the wallets, exchanges and block explorers, and publishes the EU MiCA whitepaper. pepecoin.org is the foundation’s site.

Which block explorer should I use?

Any of them: they all read the same Pepecoin blockchain. PepeBlocks also has a rich list, Pepeplorer has network stats. All five are listed on pepecoin.com.

Is Pepecoin Hub official?

No. It is a community fact site made by a regular guy from r/pepecoin. The official site is pepecoin.com.
