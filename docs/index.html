Looking for “Pepe coin”?

Three different assets go by almost the same name. Pepecoin (PEP), the one this site is about, runs on its own blockchain; the other two are tokens on Ethereum. Check the ticker and the address before you buy.

  • PEPPepecoinown proof-of-work blockchain, since 2024address starts with P
  • PEPEPepeERC-20 token on Ethereum, since 2023address starts with 0x
  • PEPECOINPepeCoinproject from 2016, an Ethereum token since 2023address starts with 0x

Buy PEP in four steps

  1. Step 1

    Open a Kraken account

    Buy on the PEP/USD or PEP/EUR market. PEP is Pepecoin with its own blockchain, not PEPE.

  2. Step 2

    Withdraw to your own wallet

    After buying, send your PEP to your own wallet (the address starts with P) and keep it safe.

  3. Step 3

    Get a Tangem, if you like

    A hardware card that keeps PEP offline. Pepecoin's official shop sells branded Tangem cards.

  4. Step 4

    HODL and say hi

    Drop by the Discord or r/pepecoin and feel the vibe.

Mine PEP in four steps

  1. Step 1

    Get a Scrypt ASIC

    The same machines that mine Litecoin and Dogecoin, such as the Antminer L9.

  2. Step 2

    Join a pool that merge-mines PEP

    ViaBTC, F2Pool, Binance Pool and others pay PEP on top of LTC and DOGE.

  3. Step 3

    Add a PEP payout address

    An address that starts with P. An Ethereum 0x address will not work.

  4. Step 4

    Point the miner at the pool

    Use the pool's Scrypt address. PEP arrives with your LTC and DOGE.

About Pepecoin

What is Pepecoin?

Pepecoin (PEP) is an open proof-of-work coin on its own blockchain — a peer-to-peer network for Pepe, with no premine and no single operator. Nodes follow a public ruleset anyone can run.

Triple mineable

Litecoin, Dogecoin and Pepecoin can be mined together on the same Scrypt work through merged mining (AuxPoW). How to mine PEP →
