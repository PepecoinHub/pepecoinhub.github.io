About Pepecoin

What is Pepecoin?

Pepecoin (PEP) is an open proof-of-work coin on its own blockchain — a peer-to-peer network for Pepe, with no premine and no single operator. Nodes follow a public ruleset anyone can run.

Triple mineable

Litecoin, Dogecoin and Pepecoin can be mined together on the same Scrypt work through merged mining (AuxPoW).
